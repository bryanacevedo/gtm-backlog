Triage as an inbox. Pick the identity.

This one is interactive. Click a request, set the inputs and watch where it lands in the queue, then press Accept. Same structure in all three; only type, color and surface change. Built to the rules from the research: no colored edge bars, no pills, no uppercase labels, and motion only when something changes.

    On a phone, the mock scrolls sideways inside its frame; the real app gets its own phone layout. Fonts are all free: Geist (Vercel's family), IBM Plex, Instrument Sans. The research flags Geist as a font AI tools now default to, which is the honest risk with A.
